How It Works
The Sealed Envelope Protocol
  1. You vote by sealing your choice as a cryptographic hash on-chain — nobody can see it
  2. The voting window closes at the deadline
  3. You reveal your vote — the contract verifies it matches your original commitment
  4. Anyone finalizes the result — trustlessly, on-chain
